## ./pentest

Security assessments for embedded devices, vehicle networks, and IoT.

- Automotive and heavy-vehicle testing: CAN, J1939, J1708/J2497, diagnostics, and ECU firmware
- Embedded and IoT devices: hardware interfaces, firmware extraction and reverse engineering, wireless
- Maritime and OT networks: NMEA 2000 and industrial protocols
- Deliverables: a findings report with reproduction steps, risk ratings, and remediation guidance

## ./prototype

Rapid prototyping, from proof of concept to working hardware.

- Embedded Linux platforms, microcontrollers, and custom tooling
- Vehicle-network test rigs and instrumentation
- Security built in from the first board, not added afterwards

## ./learn

Courses and workshops on AI/ML, taught from the embedded and security side.

- Machine learning on embedded and edge hardware
- Practical AI/ML security: attacking and defending models
- Team workshops and CTF-style training
