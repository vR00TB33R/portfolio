VPTP is an operating-system wrapper built for mobile vehicle assessment. The project uses Raspbian as its foundation for broad hardware support and combines CAN sniffing, injection, and replay with general-purpose penetration-testing tools.

The goal is a compact platform that reduces setup time during quick-turnaround automotive and transportation security work without hiding the underlying tools from the operator.
