![Electric Go-Kart](/media/projects/ekart/cover.png)

For this senior-design project, I developed the CAN communication between the electric go-kart and a Raspberry Pi 4 infotainment system. The larger platform incorporated LiDAR sensing, GPS route planning and optimization, and a custom machine-learning approach to obstacle avoidance.

The project became a practical laboratory for integration work across embedded controls, vehicle networking, perception, and operator-facing software.
